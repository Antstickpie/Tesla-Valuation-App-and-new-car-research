## 3. Photographic Forensic Analysis & Evidence

From inspecting all 15 high-resolution listing photos downloaded to the Advert repository:

### 1. Left Rear Wheel Arch & Bumper Corner Scuff (Photo 13)
* **The Damage:** A heavy graze on the black textured wheel arch cladding, accompanied by a scratch along the adjacent bumper panel edge that has cut through the clearcoat and basecoat down to primer/undercoat.
* **Repair Reality:** 
  - The plastic wheel arch trim cannot be polished; it requires replacement (Tesla OEM Part: *Wheel Arch Moulding LH*, ~€85–€110) or heat-texturing.
  - The bumper edge scratch is adjacent to the seam; a smart-repair spot paint blending costs ~€250–€300.
* **Negotiation Leverage:** Quote a professional body shop estimate of **€450–€550** to rectify both pieces.

### 2. Gemini 19" Wheels & Curb Rash (Photos 14 & 15)
* **The Damage:** Multiple rim lips exhibit clear, raw aluminum scrape marks from curb contact at the 12 o'clock and 6 o'clock positions.
* **Repair Reality:** Standard wheel repair shop charges ~€80–€100 per wheel to machine/paint, or you can fit aftermarket full-coverage black/gunmetal wheel covers (€120 for a set of 4) which clip directly over the damaged lip and hide it completely.
* **Negotiation Leverage:** Deduct **€300** for rim refurbishment.

### 3. Tire Contradiction (Goodyear vs Continental)
* **The Listing Text:** *"Am Auto sind Allwetterreifen von Conti montiert."*
* **The Photographic Evidence:** Photos 14 and 15 clearly show **Goodyear Vector 4Seasons Gen-3 SUV (255/45 R19 104W)** with the M+S symbol and 3PMSF snowflake.
* **Significance:** 
  - Either the seller confused Continental with Goodyear, or the car has mismatched tires (e.g., Contis on front, Goodyears on rear).
  - *Inspection Action:* Check all 4 tire sidewalls immediately upon arrival. If brands or tread depths are mismatched (>2 mm difference across axles), demand an additional **€200** deduction.

### 4. HEPA & Air Filter Maintenance
* **The Listing Text:** *"im September 2026 neue Scheibenwischer und alle 6 Luftfilter erneuert"*
* **Technical Note:** The Model Y has **6 filters total** (2 interior cabin pollen filters behind the center console side panel + 2 large HEPA filters and 2 pre-filters under the frunk cowl). Replacing all 6 filters costs ~€150 in parts and takes ~45 minutes of labor. This confirms conscientious preventative maintenance by the seller.

## 4. Legal, Ownership & Tax Pitfalls (Crucial for Tomorrow)

### ⚠️ Pitfall 1: "Dienstwagen" vs "Private Seller" vs 19% MwSt
The seller is listed as a private seller on AutoScout24, but explicitly writes:  
*"Ich verkaufe meinen Dienstwagen... Die MwSt ist ausweisbar"* (VAT deductible).

* **Who owns the car on paper?** Look at **Zulassungsbescheinigung Teil II (Fahrzeugbrief)**, Field C.1.1 / C.1.2:
  - Is it in the seller's personal name, or is it registered to a **GmbH, UG, GmbH & Co. KG, or Einzelunternehmen**?
  - If registered to a company: **The seller MUST be the registered managing director (Geschäftsführer) or present a written Power of Attorney (Verkaufsvollmacht) on company letterhead.**
  - If the purchase contract is made with a company, **they cannot legally exclude statutory warranty (Sachmängelhaftung) to a private buyer (§ 476 BGB)**. If they try to write "Verkauf unter Ausschluss jeglicher Gewährleistung", but issue a commercial VAT invoice, that exclusion is legally void against a consumer!
* **Invoice vs Purchase Contract:**
  - If you require the VAT (e.g. buying for your own business), you need an official **Rechnung mit ausgewiesener Mehrwertsteuer** with commercial tax ID.
  - If you are buying privately, your gross price is **€31,500**. Do NOT pay VAT on top of €31,500!

### ⚠️ Pitfall 2: Number of Previous Owners (2 Halter)
* The listing states **2 previous owners** on a car registered in 05/2023.
* **Questions to Ask:**
  1. Was owner #1 Tesla Motors (as a showroom/demo car or employee lease), and owner #2 the seller's company?
  2. Or was owner #1 a leasing company (e.g., Santander / Alphabet / LeasePlan)?
  3. If it was previously leased, verify the leasing company has issued a formal lien release (**Ablöseerklärung**) and that the seller physically has the original **Teil II (Brief)** in hand right now. **NEVER pay for a car if Teil II is still held by a bank!**

### ⚠️ Pitfall 3: Driving Home on Seller's License Plates (`M-N 3785E`)
* In Germany, driving home on the seller's plates requires an **ADAC standard purchase contract** containing the **"Veräußerungsanzeige"**:
  - The contract must record the **exact handover date and minute (e.g., 28.10.2026, 14:35 Uhr)**.
  - The seller signs, you sign. The risk and insurance liability legally transfer to the buyer from that exact minute (§ 13 Abs. 4 FZV).
  - The buyer contractually agrees to re-register / de-register the vehicle within **3 working days**.
  - **Insurance / eVB:** You should bring an **eVB-Nummer** (electronic insurance confirmation) from your own insurance company ready for immediate registration.
